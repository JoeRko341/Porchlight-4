Porchlight

A time bank for neighbors — trade help, not money. Post what you need or what you can offer, and log the hours as you go.
  1. Post a need ("could use a ride Tuesday") or an offer ("happy to help with tech setup").
  2. Someone nearby responds, you agree on a time.
  3. After you meet, whoever helped logs the hours — that's a credit they can spend later, asking someone else for help.
